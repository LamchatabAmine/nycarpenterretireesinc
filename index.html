About Us

A guild built on a lifetime of craft

For generations, the carpenters of New York City have framed its homes, raised its towers and finished the spaces where its people live and work. Their skill and dedication shaped the skyline we know today.

The Retiree's Carpenter Guild Fund was formed in 2016 under the New York Not-for-Profit Corporation Law as a charitable corporation. Based in Brooklyn, we are dedicated to the community of retired carpenters, the men and women whose careers built this city.

We are recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity, and contributions to the Fund are tax-deductible as allowed by law.

“Every building in this city carries the work of a carpenter's hands. Those hands deserve to be honored.”
Our Guiding Belief

Our Purpose

What the Guild stands for

Our work is charitable, community-centered and dedicated to those who gave their careers to the trade.

Supporting Retirees

Standing with retired carpenters and their families, so that those who served the trade are not forgotten after their working years.

Fellowship & Community

Keeping the bonds of the job site alive by bringing retirees together as one guild, a community of shared history and mutual respect.

Honoring the Craft

Preserving and celebrating the heritage of carpentry in New York City, and the contributions of the tradespeople who built it.

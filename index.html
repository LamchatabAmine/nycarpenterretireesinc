Our Mission

We Go Where
Others Can't

Cat Wrangler Rescue Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. We specialize in the rescues that no one else will take — street cats in crisis, abuse survivors, and high-risk placements that require dedicated, hands-on intervention.

⛑️

Street Rescue

We respond to emergency calls for cats living on the streets of Queens and Brooklyn, providing immediate care and safe placement.

💛

Abuse Recovery

Cats who have suffered abuse need specialized rehabilitation. Our team provides trauma-informed care to help them heal and find loving homes.

🏠

High-Risk Placements

We handle the tough cases — cats with medical needs, behavioral challenges, or those who've been overlooked by other organizations.

🌎

Queens, Brooklyn & Beyond

From Queens to Brooklyn and beyond, we go the distance when cats need us most. No distance is too great when a life is on the line.

About & Contact

Based in New York,
Reaching Beyond

Cat Wrangler Rescue Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Founded by Susan A. Raso, we are a fully grassroots, volunteer-driven operation committed to the most vulnerable cats in our communities.

We believe every cat deserves a fighting chance — regardless of how difficult their situation is. Across Queens, Brooklyn, and beyond, we go the distance to make that happen.
